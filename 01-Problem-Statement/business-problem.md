
# Business Problem Statement

## Project
Improving Swiggy Customer Support Experience

## 1. Background
Swiggy connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners through its food delivery platform. A reliable customer support experience is important when an order does not go as expected.

## 2. Problem Statement
Customers may face difficulties with:
- Delayed orders
- Incorrect or missing food items
- Order cancellations
- Refund delays
- Repeated communication with support

These experiences may increase customer frustration and reduce trust.

## 3. Business Impact
Potential business challenges:
- Increased support contact volume
- Higher support agent workload
- Longer resolution time
- Lower customer satisfaction
- Reduced customer retention

## 4. Product Opportunity
Potential improvements:
- Real-time order status updates
- Proactive delay notifications
- Context-aware self-service support
- Clear refund tracking
- Improved escalation workflows

## 5. Product Objective
Improve customer support by reducing customer effort, accelerating issue resolution, and increasing operational efficiency.

## 6. Validation Plan
Validate these hypotheses through customer interviews, support ticket analysis, and operational data.

## 7. Expected Outcomes
- Higher customer satisfaction
- Faster resolution
- Fewer repeat support contacts
- Better agent productivity

## Disclaimer
This is an independent portfolio case study and is not an official Swiggy project. The problems are hypotheses requiring validation.
