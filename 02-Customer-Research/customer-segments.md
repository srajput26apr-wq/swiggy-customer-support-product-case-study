
# Customer Segmentation – Swiggy Customer Support

## 1. Objective

Identify different customer groups based on their food delivery behavior, support expectations, and common issues.

The objective is to understand how customer support needs differ across segments and identify opportunities for better product experiences.

## 2. Customer Segments

### Segment 1: Frequent Customers

**Profile:**
- Place food orders regularly.
- Familiar with the Swiggy application.
- Expect reliable delivery and quick resolution.

**Pain Points:**
- Repeated delivery delays.
- Recurring order issues.
- Time-consuming support interactions.

**Product Opportunity:**
- Personalized support.
- Faster issue resolution.
- Proactive order notifications.

### Segment 2: First-Time Customers

**Profile:**
- New to the platform.
- May be unfamiliar with ordering and support processes.
- Need clear guidance and reassurance.

**Pain Points:**
- Confusion about order tracking.
- Uncertainty about cancellations and refunds.
- Difficulty finding support options.

**Product Opportunity:**
- Guided support journeys.
- Clear refund explanations.
- Easy-to-find help options.

### Segment 3: Time-Sensitive Customers

**Profile:**
- Order food during office breaks or busy schedules.
- Value accurate delivery estimates.

**Pain Points:**
- Unexpected delivery delays.
- Lack of timely updates.
- Difficulty communicating urgency.

**Product Opportunity:**
- Proactive delay alerts.
- Improved delivery estimates.
- Clear escalation options.

### Segment 4: Issue-Prone Customers

**Profile:**
- Have experienced missing items, incorrect orders, or cancellations.
- Need reliable issue resolution.

**Pain Points:**
- Repeated complaints.
- Unclear refund status.
- Multiple interactions to resolve one issue.

**Product Opportunity:**
- Context-aware support.
- Better complaint tracking.
- Transparent resolution timelines.

## 3. Prioritization

The initial product discovery focus will be on frequent customers and issue-prone customers because their recurring support experiences may reveal opportunities to reduce repeat contacts and improve resolution.

This prioritization is a hypothesis and requires validation using customer research and support data.

## 4. Research Questions

1. What are the most common reasons customers contact support?
2. Which issues generate the most repeat contacts?
3. How do first-time customers discover support?
4. What information do customers expect during a delay?
5. Which support issues have the greatest impact on customer satisfaction?

## 5. Conclusion

Customer segmentation helps identify different support expectations and enables the product team to design more relevant, efficient, and customer-centric support experiences.

## Disclaimer

These segments are proposed research hypotheses for an independent portfolio project. They are not based on Swiggy's internal customer data.
