# Swiggy Customer Support – Product Management Case Study

## Project Overview

This is an independent Product Management portfolio case study focused on improving the customer support experience for Swiggy users.

The project explores how product improvements, workflow automation, and better support experiences can reduce customer effort while improving operational efficiency.

## Business Problem

Customers may experience frustration when orders are delayed, cancelled, incorrectly delivered, or when refunds take longer than expected.

The key product challenge is to make customer support faster, more transparent, and more effective while managing support operations efficiently.

## Project Objectives

- Identify customer pain points across the support journey.
- Understand customer segments and user needs.
- Map the customer journey for delayed orders.
- Identify root causes behind recurring support issues.
- Recommend product solutions to improve support resolution.
- Define success metrics and a six-month product roadmap.

## Product Management Deliverables

1. Business Problem Definition
2. Business-to-Product Outcomes
3. Customer Segmentation
4. User Persona
5. Customer Journey Mapping
6. Root Cause Analysis
7. Product Recommendations
8. Six-Month Product Roadmap
9. Success Metrics and KPIs
10. Research and Validation Plan

## Key Product Metrics

- First Contact Resolution (FCR)
- Average Resolution Time
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Contact Rate per Order
- Support Automation Rate
- Customer Effort Score (CES)

## Approach

The case study follows a structured Product Management approach:

Problem Discovery → Customer Research → Root Cause Analysis → Solution Design → Prioritization → Roadmap → Measurement

## Important Note

This is an independent portfolio exercise, not an official Swiggy project.

Operational numbers, market assumptions, and proposed improvement targets are illustrative and require validation through actual customer and business data.
